NAZEEF GEMS GARDEN

A sparkling match-3 adventure! Swap colourful gems, make big matches, earn stars and unlock new levels.

💡 Make a row or column of 3 or more matching gems.
🌟 Reach the score goal before your moves run out.

🎓💎

HOW TO PLAY

Let’s learn together! Your first level is a practice level.

1Pick a gem.
Tap any gem on the board, or swipe it to a neighbour.
2Swap with a neighbour.
Swipe to a gem directly above, below, left or right — or tap two neighbours.
3Match 3 or more!
Make a row or column of the same gems to score points.
4Reach the goal.
Keep matching until you reach 500 points before your moves run out.
